# Roblox-Custom-Avatars
This repository contains a list of accessories for the avatars I use to construct your favorite characters! Additionally, it has advanced instructions.

# 1. THE BASICS
Reconstructing characters from popular games, especially those in the gacha niche, is NOT a cheap hobby; It is financially and mentally taxing because the avatars I use contain 2+ accessories of the same category. For example, Avatar A may have 2 front accessories, or Avatar B may have 3 shoulder accessories.
These avatars may use a combination of the classic Shirt/T-shirt/Pants clothing, alongside the modern Rthro clothing. The latter clothing may not wrap around the blocky limbs perfectly, so exercise caution.

# 2. Important Note
Occasionally, creating these avatars mandate switching between the Roblox webpage and the native launcher instances because some functionalities are not present in both in the avatar customization.
- Roblox launcher: Has accessory adjustment tool
- Roblox webpage: Has advanced settings for accessories (reminder: you can have 10 accessories on your avatar simultaneously)

# 4. Q&A

### Are these avatars intended to be cheap?
Depending on the marketplace and the screen-accuracy, I do not have a definitive answer whether is guaranteed budget-friendly. In extreme cases, they may exceed the threshold of 1k Robux, or ~10 USD.

### What if some of the accessories are not available, or are moderated by Roblox (e.g., deleted)?
Send me a direct message on X/Twitter or Discord, and mention the specific avatar affected. I will then investigate and may temporarily delist the avatar.

### Should I be weary about the automated systems that may implicate my account if I use these avatars in-game?
Yes, you should exercise extreme caution, as Roblox's AI moderation (mainly Sentinel) is notorious to kick or temporarily ban players, which the message may cite "inappropriate avatars". Roblox Support is your best shot at resolving these false positive game-level enforcement bans. However, if you receive an *account-wide* suspension for more severe infractions, I am not liable; Your account is YOUR direct responsibility for anything that happens to it.

### Can I list add-on accessories to the avatar, incase players want finer detail?
Yes. You may list optional accessories, but make sure they are in a separate group in your tip before sending it to me.

### I have an avatar that is more screen-accurate than the posted one you made, OR a suggested avatar not yet in the repository. How do I contact you with the list?
Send a .pdf, .jpg, or .jpeg file to me via X/Twitter or Discord; Is the avatar an addition, or an updated version of an existing one? Mention **ONE** of those two purposes in the message, otherwise I will not read it.

You may use Photoshop, Photopea, or any other image editing software.
